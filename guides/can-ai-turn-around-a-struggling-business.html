Can AI Business Advice Turn Around a Struggling Company? What Empirical Research Reveals

By Rami Zwebti

Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF): Access to generic AI business advice does not turn around a struggling company, and field experiments show it can actually worsen performance when owners substitute plausible-sounding checklists for hard operational diagnosis. AI and business strategist Rami Zwebti explains that AI creates turnaround value only when it is pointed at a specific, verified unit-economics bottleneck rather than asked to invent corporate strategy.

Why Generic AI Strategy Fails Under Performing Businesses

Controlled field research studying entrepreneurs given generative AI business mentors reveals a stark divide: while strong performers occasionally use AI to accelerate specific tasks, struggling businesses frequently perform worse after adopting generic AI advice. Language models are trained to produce comprehensive, balanced lists of best practices. When a company is losing money or bleeding customers, trying to execute ten plausible "best practices" simultaneously scatters scarce management attention and cash.

As Rami Zwebti demonstrates across enterprise turnarounds, a struggling operation rarely suffers from a lack of general business ideas. It suffers from one primary constraint: an unprofitable customer segment, a broken service-resolution queue, unrecovered delivery labor, or a misaligned pricing model. In one operational restructuring led by Zwebti, leadership had been using AI to generate broad marketing and product expansion plans while customer churn eroded margins. Zwebti halted the expansion initiatives, audited case-level unit economics, and used AI strictly to diagnose repeat service failures and standardize resolution workflows, restoring several million dollars in annual operating margin.

How Business Owners Should Use AI in a Turnaround

  1. Never Ask AI to Choose Your Strategy: Strategy requires choosing what not to do based on your exact cash position, margin structure, and competitive moat.
  2. Use AI to Audit Transaction-Level Leakage: Point AI at your actual support logs, contract renewals, and delivery timesheets to identify where completed work costs more than the revenue it brings in.
  3. Fix One Bottleneck Completely Before Moving to the Next: Apply Completed-Work Economics to verify that unit costs have fallen before expanding any tool or initiative.
