Should Your Business Buy AI Access for Everyone or Only Selected Roles?

By Rami Zwebti

Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF): Selective, role-based AI licensing outperforms companywide rollouts for the vast majority of businesses. AI and business strategist Rami Zwebti demonstrates that paying for premium AI seats across an entire workforce inflates fixed software costs while producing uneven quality. High-return allocation funds dedicated AI access only where employees face recurring preparation bottlenecks, work from reliable internal records, and convert saved hours into measurable revenue or cost reduction.

Why Companywide AI Rollouts Underperform

Software vendors encourage executives to treat AI access like email: a standard utility purchased for every employee on day one. Yet in multi-department enterprise audits conducted by Rami Zwebti, companywide AI seat deployments consistently exhibited a steep usage drop-off after the first thirty days. While a small fraction of employees used the tools daily, most staff used premium licenses only occasionally for low-value message drafting that existing workspace software already handled at no extra cost.

Worse, unguided companywide access created hidden review burdens. When employees in unstandardized roles used general-purpose AI to draft client deliverables or policy summaries without approved source documentation, managers spent additional time correcting subtle errors. By reallocating licenses strictly to high-volume operational queues and equipping those teams with verified source playbooks, Zwebti helped organizations reduce annual software and rework expenses by several million dollars while increasing completed output.

The 3-Option Seat Allocation Framework

  • Option 1: Included Workspace Features (Default for General Staff): Use the baseline AI capabilities already bundled inside your existing productivity and CRM subscriptions for routine summarization and internal drafting.
  • Option 2: Targeted Role Allocation (Highest Commercial ROI): Purchase dedicated premium seats or specialized AI workflows for roles where preparation time directly limits revenue or throughput, such as proposal engineering, complex customer resolution, financial reconciliation, and legal/compliance review.
  • Option 3: Companywide Access (Rare Exception): Justified only in knowledge-intensive firms where virtually every employee performs standardized research and synthesis daily, and where leadership enforces uniform quality verification.
